About DrugBug

A medication study app built by a Paramedic, FP-C, for everyone who has to know their meds, and for anyone who just wants to understand the bottle in their cabinet.

Our story

I kept coming across medications I don’t normally use, and I wanted a way to make them feel familiar. So I started building something that would put them in front of me often enough to get comfortable with them.

The further I got, the more it became something I needed to share. DrugBug began to hatch.

DrugBug explains a drug from every angle: what it does inside the body, how it works, how it is given, what it is used for, what to watch for afterward, and what it can be confused with. It is written in plain words first, so the explanation makes sense before the clinical language does.

The whole app is tailored to your role. A CNA, an EMT, a Paramedic, an LVN, an RN, a flight crew member, a medical student and an imaging tech open the same drug and read it written for the work that person actually does, at the depth their scope calls for, with questions set to that level.

It is built for everyday people too. If you are not in medicine at all and you just want to understand the bottle in your cabinet — what it is for, how it works, what it does not mix with, what the side effects mean — there is a whole side of DrugBug written for exactly that, in language anybody can follow. Doses are cited to the FDA label.

— Adam, Paramedic, FP-C

DrugBug is for study only. It is not a medical device and is not for clinical decision-making or patient care.

Why DrugBug

Learn by scope

A CNA, an EMT, a Paramedic, an RN, a medical student and an imaging tech each get the drugs, depth and questions their own role calls for.

Plain words first

Every drug is explained in everyday language before the clinical version, so the explanation makes sense before the vocabulary does.

Every angle of a drug

What it does in the body, how it works, how it is given, what it is used for, what to watch for and what it can be confused with.

Careful about sources

Doses are cited to the FDA label. Questions are reviewed and graded by our evaluators, and anything that breaks a rule is pulled until it is fixed.

Study, not patient care

DrugBug is for education only. It is not a medical device and never tells anyone to take, give or use a drug. Your protocols and medical direction decide care.

Fun keeps you coming back

A bug that grows as you learn, sticks to earn and bug music to study to. Prefer it plain? Focused mode turns all of that off.

Meet the characters

Hickory

Your guide. Hickory walks you through each level and runs L.E.A.R.N: every drug in your scope, one at a time, in random order, until you have seen them all.

Professor Sage

Every drug gets explained twice: once clinically, and once by Sage, in words anybody can follow. He also says what a lab value really means and the mistake people usually make.

Your bug

Every level comes with its own bug, from the Siren Beetle to the Monarch Nurse. It grows from Rookie to Legend as you learn, and you can collect more than 150 others.
