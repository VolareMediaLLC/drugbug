Privacy Policy

Volare Media LLC · Effective 2026-11-11

Who we are

Volare Media LLC ("Volare," "we," or "us") operates DrugBug, the medication study app and website at drugbugapp.com. This policy explains how we handle personal information when you use DrugBug in the United States, including on the web and in the iPhone app. Some features and purchase options are available only on certain platforms.

Information we collect and why

Device and study information. DrugBug can be used without an account. It stores settings, study progress, a saved study list, score history, virtual items and an agreement record on your device or in your browser. Certain features send information to our systems using a device, account or class identifier. We use this information to operate the features you use, maintain access and protect the service. When you accept the Terms, we record the version, date and time, and your device or account identifier on our systems to maintain a record of acceptance.

Optional accounts. When you create an account by email or use Sign in with Apple, we receive an account identifier and email address, which may be an Apple private relay address. We store study data so you can sync it across devices and use account features. Firebase Authentication handles email/password authentication. Apple handles your Apple Account credentials. We use the account and entitlement information to administer the one-time, three-day Pro access for new accounts. The offer does not require payment details and does not renew.

Usage statistics. DrugBug collects optional usage statistics only after you choose to share them. We record these statistics under a randomly generated device identifier. These include active days and approximate time, sections and features used, counts of answered and correct questions, first difficulty ratings, study cards viewed, plan, study route and level, version and platform. We use the statistics to understand usage, measure upgrades and improve study content. A random identifier can distinguish one device from another, so we treat device-level statistics as potentially identifiable even when a name or email is absent. If you used DrugBug before the effective date of this policy, the update turned off optional statistics and asked you to choose whether to share them. We deleted the device-level usage statistics collected before this change. We delete or aggregate device-level usage statistics within 24 months after collection. Turn off optional statistics under Share usage statistics in Settings. Use Your data or contact us to request deletion of stored statistics associated with your device, where we can locate them. Turning off optional statistics does not stop records needed for an account, purchase, agreement, class or other feature you use.

Purchases. On the website, Stripe processes subscription and stick-pack purchases. Apple processes purchases made through the App Store. We use the purchase and subscription records made available to us, such as the plan, its status and renewal or expiration date, or the pack purchased, to provide access, administer billing and handle purchase support. The payment provider handles your full payment card number. It may collect other information under its own privacy policy.

Codes. For codes that require named redemption, such as gift, access, evaluator, school or agency license, and protocol codes, we collect your full name and associate it with the redemption and, where applicable, your account. We use that information to verify eligibility, administer access and prevent misuse. A student class key does not ask for your name. An institutional license or protocol code can be a named code even if it is supplied by a school or agency.

Evaluator records. When enrollment is available, if you submit an evaluator application, we collect your name, email, credentials or role, years of experience, workplace and the information you choose to provide so we can evaluate and respond to the application. Evaluator records may include an evaluator code, typed signature, agreement choices and version, date and time, question ratings and votes, comments, questions viewed and activity counts. We use records from the program to administer participation, maintain agreement records and review and improve study content. Other users may see an aggregate difficulty rating or a content review indicator. We will seek permission before publicly crediting an evaluator by name.

Instructors and organization contacts. We collect an instructor's name, work or account email, school or agency, classes, class key labels, nickname approvals and class results. Instructors can sign in with email/password or Google. If you contact us about the instructor pilot or an organizational package, we receive the details you provide, such as your name, organization, work email, optional phone number and message. We use these records to manage accounts and classes, respond and prepare a quote when requested.

Support and feedback. If you email us or submit an error report, feedback or a protocol request, we receive the information you choose to send and any reply address the form or email supplies. We use it to respond, resolve issues, assess requests and improve DrugBug. Do not include patient data, another person's personal information or any document you are unauthorized to share.

Technical information. Our hosting and service providers receive standard information needed to serve the app or site, such as an IP address when a page loads. We use local storage and similar technology to remember settings, maintain sessions and run features. Optional usage statistics measure activity within DrugBug across sessions. We do not sell personal information, share it for cross-context behavioral advertising or use it for targeted advertising. DrugBug does not track users over time and across third-party websites or apps, and we do not allow third parties to do so through DrugBug. DrugBug therefore does not change its practices in response to browser Do Not Track signals.

Classes and organization programs

A class key identifies your activity to the instructor using a class alias such as "Student 07." Your instructor can see question and accuracy counts by medication, drug class, body system, question style, study mode and drill topic, as well as the missed question identifiers, the answers you selected and when you last studied. Free-text responses are excluded from the class performance report. An instructor may be able to identify you through a separate key sheet the instructor maintains or other information available to the instructor. Do not send a class roster or a key sheet to DrugBug.

Where a scoreboard is enabled and its use is authorized, classmates or participating members can see the displayed alias or instructor-approved nickname and relevant scores or rankings. Agency protocol competitions may display team names, team rankings and individual rankings to participants. Choose a nickname that does not identify you or another student. Class prizes may be saved to your device or account. The public leaderboard and interschool competitions are currently disabled.

We use class records to provide and administer the authorized class features. When we handle student records on behalf of a school under a written agreement, we follow the school's authorized instructions and the applicable agreement and law. Contact your instructor or school for access to or correction of school-controlled records. You may also contact us for help directing a request. Leaving a class, closing an account and deleting a class are separate actions. Records held by a school or instructor are subject to that organization's policies and applicable law.

Who receives information

  • Service providers receive the information needed for the services they provide. We require third parties to which we provide app user data for processing on our behalf to protect that data to at least the standard described in this policy and required by Apple's App Review Guidelines. Google Firebase and Google Cloud process account, study and device information to provide authentication, database hosting and server functions. GitHub Pages receives web request information to deliver the website. Google's Gmail service receives messages and reply information when you email our contact address. Use that address for general inquiries and to initiate a privacy request. Do not send identifiable patient information or school-controlled records to that address. Google or Apple handles the relevant sign-in information when you choose its sign-in service. Apple also handles app distribution and App Store purchases. Stripe handles website purchases. Apple, Stripe and other providers may also process information for their own services under their own privacy policies.
  • Instructors, classmates and participants may receive class or competition information as described above. We do not display named redemption records or account emails on class scoreboards.
  • We may disclose relevant information to comply with law, valid legal process or a lawful government request, or to investigate fraud, address a security issue or protect rights or safety. If Volare is involved in a proposed or completed sale, reorganization or similar transaction, we may disclose information as needed for that transaction subject to appropriate confidentiality and applicable law.

Patient information and personal health details

DrugBug is for education. The saved list is for medications you are studying. Do not use it to record a person's medication regimen or to check for interactions. Do not submit identifiable patient information, personal symptoms, diagnoses, medical records or other personal health details through any feature, form or message. If you believe you sent this information in error, contact us without repeating the information in your request.

Retention and security

We keep personal information for as long as reasonably needed for the purposes described in this policy. We consider the type of record, the feature it supports and legal, security or recordkeeping requirements. For example, purchase and assent records may be kept for billing, accounting, legal compliance and dispute resolution. If we retain information after a deletion request, we limit its use to the reason for retaining it. Backup copies may remain until they are removed under the backup cycle. A school's instructions and applicable law may govern retention or return of school-controlled class records. We may keep aggregate or properly de-identified information. When we maintain data as de-identified, we take reasonable steps to prevent re-identification and do not attempt to re-identify it except where permitted by law to test whether it is properly de-identified. Where required, we require recipients of that data to honor the same restrictions.

We use safeguards designed to protect the information we hold, including restricted administrative access and encryption in transit. No method of storage or transmission can eliminate every risk.

Choices and privacy requests

Use Share usage statistics in Settings to turn optional statistics off. If you have an account, use Download my data in Settings to access the available export. Use Your data or the account settings in the app to start deleting your account, or email VolareMediaLLC@gmail.com for help. You can use the same email address to ask about information associated with your account or device. Removing the app or clearing browser storage can remove records stored locally. It does not send us a request to delete online records. Account deletion also does not cancel a paid subscription. Manage an App Store subscription through Apple and a web subscription through Manage subscription on the website.

Depending on applicable law, you may have the right to confirm processing, access and obtain a portable copy, correct or delete personal information, learn about third-party disclosures, withdraw consent, or opt out of certain sales, sharing, targeted advertising or profiling. We do not use personal information for profiling in furtherance of decisions that produce legal or similarly significant effects. To start a privacy request, email VolareMediaLLC@gmail.com. Do not email sensitive records or identity documents. If we need to verify your identity or authority, we will direct you to a separate verification channel and request only information reasonably necessary for that purpose. We will explain any decision to decline a request as required by law. An authorized agent may submit a request where permitted by law. If an appeal is available, email the same address with "Privacy appeal" in the subject line. We will respond within the applicable period and tell you how to contact the appropriate regulator when required. We will not unlawfully discriminate against you for exercising your rights.

Age, policy updates and contact

DrugBug is for people age 18 and older. If you believe a minor has provided personal information, email us so we can investigate and take appropriate action, including deletion where required.

The effective date appears at the top of this policy. We will post updates here and give notice of material changes through the service or another appropriate channel. Where applicable law requires consent for a new use, we will obtain it separately before processing information for that use.
