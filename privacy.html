Privacy Policy

Effective 2026-11-09. Draft pending legal review.

What DrugBug stores

On your device: your settings (theme, level), study progress, saved drug list, wardrobe and virtual items, agreement record, score history, and a random device ID used for usage statistics.

In DrugBug's online database (Google Firebase), linked to a random device ID or an anonymous account ID, never to your name or email unless you create an account or redeem a code:

  • Usage statistics, described in the next section. These include your first difficulty rating of a question, without your name.
  • If you create an account: your email address and your study data, so it syncs across your devices.
  • If you subscribe or buy sticks on the website: which plan you have, when it renews or ends, and which packs you bought. Payment is handled by Stripe.
  • If you redeem a code: your full name, with that redemption (see Codes).
  • If you join a class with a class key: that key's results and, if you choose one, your scoreboard nickname (see Students and schools).
  • If you are an evaluator: your ratings, votes and comments on questions, and which questions you have seen (see Evaluators).
  • If you are an instructor: see Instructors.
  • If you report an error or request protocols: what you write, and an email address only if you choose to give one.

Usage statistics

We collect anonymous usage statistics to understand how DrugBug is used and to improve it. They include: a random device ID that the app creates (not your name, email, Apple ID or an advertising ID); if you are signed in, a scrambled one-way code made from your account, so one person using DrugBug on more than one device is counted once (the code cannot be turned back into your account or email); the days you use DrugBug; about how many minutes you are active; which sections you open and which features you use; how many questions you answer and how many are correct; your first 1 to 5 difficulty rating of each question you rate; L.E.A.R.N cards you go through; your plan (Free, Plus or Pro); your study route and level; the app version; and whether you use the website or the iPhone app.

We use them only to measure and improve DrugBug, for example which sections are used, how hard each question is, whether people come back, and how many free users upgrade. We do not use them for advertising, we do not sell or share them, we do not combine them with information from other companies, and we do not track you across other apps or websites.

They are kept for up to 24 months, then deleted or combined into totals that cannot be tied to a device. You can turn them off at any time in Settings, Account & privacy, Share usage statistics. Turning them off also deletes the statistics stored for that device.

Optional account

DrugBug works without an account. If you choose to create one (Sign in with Apple or email and password), we store your email address (or Apple's private relay address if you use Hide My Email), an account ID, and your study data so it can sync across your devices.

Passwords and sign-in are handled by our authentication provider (Google Firebase Authentication); DrugBug never sees or stores your password, your Apple Account password or your Google password. Apple sign-in tokens are verified by the provider's servers.

Your account data is private to you: other users never see your email, account ID or study records. Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS/TLS) and at rest by our provider. Access to the database is restricted by server-side security rules, and only authorized DrugBug administrators can manage content.

You can export your data (Settings, Download my data) and delete your account in the app (Settings, Privacy & account). Deleting your account deletes your account and its study records; copies in our provider's backups expire within 30 days.

What we do not collect

We do not ask for patient information, and you must never enter it. We do not collect your contacts, location, photos or advertising ID, and we do not collect your name unless you redeem a code, sign up as an evaluator or use DrugBug as an instructor. We do not sell personal information, and we do not show ads.

Codes

When you redeem a code (a gift, Pro, evaluator, sticks, bug, school or agency license, or agency protocol code), DrugBug asks for your full name. It is saved with that redemption and on your account, and only the DrugBug team sees it, so we know who used each code. School class keys never ask for a name. Your name is deleted with your account.

Evaluators

If you sign up to evaluate questions (drugbugapp.com/evaluate.html), we keep the name, email, role, years of experience, workplace and anything else you write there, and use them only to review your sign-up and contact you about evaluating.

When you sign the evaluator agreement, we keep the name you typed as your signature, the boxes you ticked, the agreement version and the date and time. While you evaluate, your difficulty ratings, Good or Bad votes, comments, the list of questions you have seen and how many questions you grade each week are stored with your evaluator code and name. Only the DrugBug team sees them with your name. Other users see only the result: a question going live, a check mark that an evaluator has seen it, and an average difficulty.

Students and schools

A school or agency can give students anonymous class keys. If you redeem one, your results are reported to your instructor under the key's label, for example "Student 07", never under your name: how many questions you answered and how many were correct, by medication, drug class, body system, question style, study mode and drill topic; which questions you got wrong and which wrong answer you chose; and when you last studied. Only counts and question identifiers are sent, never anything you type. DrugBug does not know who holds which key; only your instructor's own key sheet does.

Your class has a scoreboard. Classmates see your alias (for example "Student 07") or a nickname you choose, and your class scores. A nickname is checked automatically for offensive words and shown only after your instructor approves it; do not use your full real name. Class prizes you win are saved on your device and account.

Deleting the class deletes every key, nickname and result. Schools that need a data agreement can contact us.

Instructors

If you use DrugBug as an instructor (signing in with email and password or with Google), we store your email address and name, your school or agency, your classes, their key labels, the nicknames you approve, and class results. If you ask to join the instructor pilot by email, we keep your message so we can reply. If you ask about a package on the Protocols page, we keep your organization, name, work email, phone (if given) and what you wrote, and use them only to reply and prepare a quote. We use this only to run your classes and to contact you about DrugBug.

Who can see it

Your progress and score history are private to your account. Usage statistics are seen only by the DrugBug team, as totals and trends. In a class, your instructor sees your key's totals and classmates see the class scoreboard, as described above. Names on codes, evaluator sign-ups and ratings, error reports and protocol requests are seen only by the DrugBug team. The public leaderboard and competitions between schools are currently turned off; if they are turned on, display names, school or team names and scores are visible to other participants.

Account deletion and retention

Use Your data (in the app's footer) to delete everything DrugBug stores about you, including this device's usage statistics, or email us. We delete or de-identify your information promptly, except where we must keep limited records for legal reasons (for example records of purchases, or a record that you agreed to the Terms). Ratings an evaluator gave may be kept without their name after deletion. Data stored only on your device is removed when you delete the app or clear this site's data.

Age

DrugBug is for adults 18 and older only. If you believe someone under 18 has used DrugBug, contact us and we will delete the data.

Your choices and rights

Turn usage statistics off in Settings, Account & privacy, Share usage statistics. Open Your data (in the app's footer) to leave the leaderboard or a class, or delete everything DrugBug stores about you. You can also email VolareMediaLLC@gmail.com to ask what we hold or to request deletion. Depending on where you live, you may have additional privacy rights; we will honor them.

Subscriptions and payments

On the website, DrugBug Plus, Pro and stick packs are sold through Stripe. Stripe processes the payment under its own privacy policy; DrugBug never receives your full card details. We only learn which plan is active, when it renews or ends, and which packs you bought.

In the iPhone app, DrugBug Plus and Pro are sold through Apple's App Store. Apple processes the payment; DrugBug never receives your card or billing details. We only learn which plan is active and when it renews or ends. Manage or cancel those subscriptions in iPhone Settings, your name, Subscriptions.

Service providers

DrugBug's database, sign-in, usage statistics and server functions are hosted by Google Firebase (Google Cloud). Apple distributes the iPhone app and handles its purchases.

The website is hosted on GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc.), which receives standard request information such as your IP address when pages load. Website payments are handled by Stripe.

Email you send us is handled by Google (Gmail). DrugBug contains no advertising and no third-party analytics or tracking tools; usage statistics are collected by DrugBug itself.
