Privacy Policy

Effective 2026-11-05. Draft pending legal review.

What DrugBug stores

On your device: your settings (theme, level), study progress, saved drug list, agreement record, score history, and a random device ID used for usage statistics.

In DrugBug's online database (Google Firebase), linked to a random device ID or an anonymous account ID, never to your name or email unless you create an account:

  • Usage statistics, described in the next section.
  • If you create an account: your email address and your study data, so it syncs across your devices.
  • If you subscribe on the website: which plan you have and when it renews or ends. Payment is handled by Stripe.
  • If you redeem a class key from a school or agency: running quiz totals for that key, described under Students and schools.
  • If you report an error or request protocols: what you write, and an email address only if you choose to give one.

Usage statistics

We collect anonymous usage statistics to understand how DrugBug is used and to improve it. They include: a random device ID that the app creates (not your name, email, Apple ID or an advertising ID); the days you use DrugBug; about how many minutes you are active; which sections you open and which features you use; how many questions you answer and how many are correct; L.E.A.R.N cards you go through; your plan (Free, Plus or Pro); your study route and level; the app version; and whether you use the website or the iPhone app.

We use them only to measure and improve DrugBug, for example which sections are used, whether people come back, and how many free users upgrade. We do not use them for advertising, we do not sell or share them, we do not combine them with information from other companies, and we do not track you across other apps or websites.

They are kept for up to 24 months, then deleted or combined into totals that cannot be tied to a device. You can turn them off at any time in Settings, Account & privacy, Share usage statistics. Turning them off also deletes the statistics stored for that device.

Optional account

DrugBug works without an account. If you choose to create one (Sign in with Apple or email and password), we store your email address (or Apple's private relay address if you use Hide My Email), an account ID, and your study data so it can sync across your devices.

Passwords and sign-in are handled by our authentication provider (Google Firebase Authentication); DrugBug never sees or stores your password or your Apple Account password. Apple sign-in tokens are verified by the provider's servers.

Your account data is private to you: other users never see your email, account ID or study records. Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS/TLS) and at rest by our provider. Access to the database is restricted by server-side security rules, and only authorized DrugBug administrators can manage content.

You can export your data (Settings, Download my data) and delete your account in the app (Settings, Privacy & account). Deleting your account deletes your account and its study records; copies in our provider's backups expire within 30 days.

What we do not collect

We do not ask for patient information, and you must never enter it. We do not collect your name, contacts, location, photos or advertising ID. We do not sell personal information, and we do not show ads.

Who can see it

Your progress and score history are private to your account. Usage statistics are seen only by the DrugBug team, as totals and trends. (The leaderboard and school competitions are currently turned off.) If turned on: leaderboard and competition display names, school or team names and scores are visible to other participants. Use a first name or nickname only. Error reports and protocol requests are seen by the DrugBug team.

Students and schools

A school or agency can give students anonymous class keys. If you redeem one, your quiz totals by medication system (questions answered, how many were correct, and when you last studied) are reported to your instructor under the key's label, for example "Student 07", never under your name. DrugBug does not know who holds which key; only your instructor's own key sheet does. Deleting the class deletes every key and its results. Schools that need a data agreement can contact us.

Account deletion and retention

Use Your data (in the app\'s footer) to delete everything DrugBug stores about you, including this device's usage statistics, or email us. We delete or de-identify your information promptly, except where we must keep limited records for legal reasons (for example records of purchases, or a record that you agreed to the Terms). Data stored only on your device is removed when you delete the app or clear this site's data.

Age

DrugBug is for adults 18 and older only. If you believe someone under 18 has used DrugBug, contact us and we will delete the data.

Your choices and rights

Turn usage statistics off in Settings, Account & privacy, Share usage statistics. Open Your data (in the app\'s footer) to leave the leaderboard or a school competition, or delete everything DrugBug stores about you. You can also email VolareMediaLLC@gmail.com to ask what we hold or to request deletion. Depending on where you live, you may have additional privacy rights; we will honor them.

Subscriptions and payments

On the website, DrugBug Plus and Pro are sold through Stripe. Stripe processes the payment under its own privacy policy; DrugBug never receives your full card details. We only learn which plan is active and when it renews or ends.

In the iPhone app, DrugBug Plus and Pro are sold through Apple's App Store. Apple processes the payment; DrugBug never receives your card or billing details. We only learn which plan is active and when it renews or ends. Manage or cancel those subscriptions in iPhone Settings, your name, Subscriptions.

Service providers

DrugBug's database, sign-in, usage statistics and server functions are hosted by Google Firebase (Google Cloud). Apple distributes the iPhone app and handles its purchases. The website is hosted on GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc.), which receives standard request information such as your IP address when pages load. Website payments are handled by Stripe. DrugBug contains no advertising and no third-party analytics or tracking tools; usage statistics are collected by DrugBug itself.
