Free DrugBug Pro

Want DrugBug for free? Help us check our questions.

DrugBug evaluators read our practice questions, rate how hard they are and tell us when something is wrong. In return, evaluators get DrugBug Pro while they evaluate. It's unpaid volunteer work, with a minimum of 40 graded questions a week.

What you do

  • Study in DrugBug like normal.
  • After each question, rate it 1 to 5 and mark it Good or Bad.
  • Check new pilot questions before students see them.
  • Suggest better wording when something is off.

Who we are looking for

EMTs, paramedics, nurses, physicians, pharmacists, imaging techs, respiratory therapists, instructors and students in those programs. If you study or work with medications, we would like to hear from you.

What you get

  • DrugBug Pro while you evaluate (at least 40 graded questions a week).
  • An Evaluator badge in the app.
  • Analytics on how everyone does with each medication.
  • A say in the questions every student sees.
