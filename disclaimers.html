DrugBug does not provide medical advice, individual recommendations, diagnosis or treatment. Using DrugBug or contacting Volare Media LLC does not establish a healthcare provider-patient relationship.

Do not use DrugBug to assess a patient, choose a medication or dose, administer a drug, interpret an individual result, select a destination, decide whether to seek care or make any other clinical or personal healthcare decision. Do not start, stop, change or combine any medication based on DrugBug. For personal medical questions, contact a licensed healthcare professional or pharmacist. In a U.S. emergency, call 911. Our feedback and support channels are for product questions and reports and are not emergency services.

Simulations, drug information and study lists

Megacode, On Scene, Big Calls and other scenarios use fictional patients for practice. Megacode uses fixed cases and does not accept patient values or calculate a dose for a real patient. Any calculations elsewhere in study exercises are for practice only. Do not enter or submit real patient information anywhere in DrugBug. DrugBug is not designed or offered as clinical decision-support software, a point-of-care reference or a medical device. It has not been cleared or approved by the U.S. Food and Drug Administration (FDA).

My List is a study list. It does not analyze anyone's medications or check for drug interactions. Drug pages may discuss interactions described in labeling, and study questions may test that material. A comparison or discussion of drug families or drug classes is educational and does not recommend a substitution for a person. Content about over-the-counter medicines, minor illnesses, vitamins, vital signs, labs and label warnings is for study. It does not assess symptoms, determine whether a result is normal for you or recommend self-treatment. Where DrugBug includes pages about illicit substances and unapproved products, those pages describe effects and risks for education. They do not encourage use or provide dosing, sourcing or use instructions.

Sources, review and updates

DrugBug uses FDA prescribing information and other sources for study content. Source dates and review indicators provide context for the material shown. A review reflects a check at a point in time. Content may be incomplete, inaccurate or out of date even after review. DrugBug uses AI to help draft some material and regularly check for FDA labeling changes. AI can introduce or miss information, and a change may appear in DrugBug after the FDA publishes it. A description of off-label use is for education and does not recommend that use. Read the cited source to verify study information. For patient care, rely on current authoritative references, applicable protocols, medical direction, facility policy and qualified clinical judgment independently of DrugBug.

Content marked Draft has not completed DrugBug's content review. Content without that label may still contain errors. Pilot questions are restricted to authorized reviewers until approved for release. An evaluator label or difficulty rating describes a content review step or user feedback and does not establish clinical validation, official exam difficulty or accuracy. Device-generated pronunciations may be inaccurate. Use Report an error to flag a suspected error without including patient information.
