Learn your meds by scope.

DrugBug is a medication study app for EMS, nursing, medical school and imaging students, built by a paramedic, FP-C.

Try it free in your browser Coming soon to iPhone

BugDex

All 2,000+ medications in one place, explained in plain language, with drug families compared side by side.

Prehospital

EMT, AEMT, Paramedic and Flight scopes, registry-style exam prep, blood products and hemodynamics.

Nursing

CNA, LVN/LPN and RN, specialty units from ICU and ER to NICU and Flight Nurse, licensure-style prep.

Medical School

Mechanisms to management with Step 1, Step 2 CK and Step 3-style practice and advanced labs.

Imaging

X-ray, CT, MRI, nuclear medicine and IR: contrast agents, reactions, eGFR and radiopharmaceuticals.

The Wormhole

Go down the wormhole on any drug or condition: where it came from and how it works inside you, from the cell to each organ. Pro, with a free preview.

3D molecules

See the chemical structure of hundreds of drugs, then tap it to spin a 3D model. Turn the hydrogens on or off and zoom in close.

Grow your bug

Earn sticks for every right answer and build your Terrarium of 90+ bugs, faces and gear.
