Questions at your level

Every question carries a one-to-four star difficulty tied to your role, so a CNA is not answering flight questions and a flight nurse is not answering CNA ones. DrugBug tracks the ones you have already done and can serve only the ones you have not seen.

Exam prep

NREMT, NCLEX, Step and registry-style practice: single answer, select-all and put-in-priority-order questions, Exam mode, weak-spot drills and a test-day countdown with a daily goal.

My List

Save the drugs you are studying and see, in depth, how they interact with each other.

Compounds and probiotics

The interesting compounds in vegetables, fruits and vitamins, on flip cards with a Surprise me button, plus probiotics.

The Wormhole and the Disease Lab

Go down the wormhole on any drug, or step into the Disease Lab on 250 conditions: where it came from and how it works inside you, from the cell to each organ, with the subtypes of each condition broken out.

Study Tools

Questions, Megacode with a live monitor and 24 cases, flashcards, Brain Games, safety drills and the Medication Disease Identifier, which reads a practice medication list back to you.

3D molecules

See the chemical structure of hundreds of drugs, then tap it to spin a 3D model. Turn the hydrogens on or off and zoom in close.

Who makes this

I kept coming across medications I don’t normally use, and I wanted a way to make them feel familiar. So I started building something that would put them in front of me often enough to get comfortable with them.

The further I got, the more it became something I needed to share. DrugBug began to hatch.

DrugBug tries to explain a drug from every angle — what it does in the body, how it works, what to watch for, and where it is used — in plain language, before anything else. Doses are cited to the FDA label, with that label’s date shown on the page, and every drug page and every question has a Report an error button. Every report is read.

— Adam, Paramedic, FP-C

DrugBug is for study only. It is not a medical device and is not for clinical decision-making or patient care.

Common questions

Do I need to install anything to try it?

No. Open it in your browser, pick a route and start. No account, no download.

Who is it for?

EMT, AEMT, Paramedic, Flight Paramedic, CNA, LVN/LPN, RN, Flight Nurse, medical students through Step 3, and imaging technologists in X-ray, CT, MRI, nuclear medicine and IR.

Can I use it for patient care?

No. DrugBug is for education only. Always follow your protocols, medical direction, facility policy and current references.

Where do the doses come from?

FDA labeling, with the label and its date shown on the drug page. Anything outside the label is marked off-label and attributed.

Is there a free version?

Yes. About 40 medications with flashcards, the daily 5-minute DrugBug, the Small Sickness Guide, normal vitals and labs, and a preview of the Wormhole and the Disease Lab, free forever.
