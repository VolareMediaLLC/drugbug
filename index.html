Pick your path

Nursing

RN, LVN/LPN and CNA

The drugs nurses give and monitor at your license, with NCLEX-style practice in the same question formats as the real exam.

  • NCLEX-RN and NCLEX-PN-style prep with select-all-that-apply and put-in-order questions
  • Specialty Units: ICU, ER, cardiac, L&D, NICU, peds, psych, oncology, dialysis and more
  • Nursing Bug drills: assess, hold, labs and patient teaching, one skill at a time
  • Lab values tied to the meds you give, plus Calc Lab dose and drip math
  • CNA exam prep focused on what CNAs need to know
EMS

EMT, AEMT and Paramedic

Registry-style prep in the formats the National Registry uses: 45% clinical judgment, and select-all questions with any number of right answers.

  • 52 unfolding calls: follow one patient from dispatch and scene size-up to handoff
  • Extra scene size-up practice for EMTs
  • 500+ clinical judgment scenarios, plus select-all and put-in-order questions
  • Dose Cards, Calc Lab, Megacode and pediatric dosing practice
  • Going further? Flight and critical care certification prep, blood products and hemodynamics
Medical school

MS1–MS2, MS3–MS4 and Intern

From mechanisms to management, with board-style practice for each stage of training.

  • Step 1, Step 2 CK and Step 3-style practice questions with Exam mode
  • Body Systems: tap a part of the body to see the drugs that act on it and the drugs that harm it
  • Drug Alternatives: can't use a drug? See same-class options and other drugs for the same problem
  • 84 lab values with interpretation, patterns and calculations
  • Hemodynamics, board-focus pages and clinical scenarios
  • Imported Decks Coming soon: bring in flashcard decks you exported from Anki and study them in DrugBug, with the drugs on each card linked to their DrugBug pages
Imaging

Radiography, CT, MRI, nuclear medicine and IR

The contrast and medication knowledge imaging technologists need, with practice for imaging certification exams.

  • Imaging exam prep with timed practice tests and scores
  • Contrast agents, screening, reactions and the reaction cart
  • Kidney function and eGFR practice, plus gadolinium (GBCA) groups for MRI
  • Radiopharmaceuticals and stress agents for nuclear medicine
  • IR safety and the medicines used during procedures
Everyone

Everyday Health: just curious?

No medical background needed. Plain-language answers about the medicines in your cabinet and how your body works.

  • Medicine Cabinet: over-the-counter remedies and vitamins, what each is labeled for and what to watch for
  • Small Sickness Guide: colds, heartburn, rashes and more, with self-care facts and when to see a doctor
  • Your Body & Medicines: tap a body part to see the medicines that work there and the ones that can harm it
  • Normal Vitals & Labs: what normal heart rate, blood pressure and lab results look like at every age
  • Conditions & Medicine Cautions: pick a condition, like high blood pressure or pregnancy, to see which medicines mention it in their warnings
  • Probiotics, peptides, how recreational drugs affect the body, and Weird Drug Wednesday

Already have Anki decks? Bring them with you.

Export a deck from Anki as an .apkg file, pick it in DrugBug, and study it right next to your DrugBug drugs.

  • Private to you. Your deck is read on your phone and saved only there. It is never uploaded or shared.
  • Drugs linked. When a card mentions a drug in DrugBug, tap it to open that drug's page.
  • Study by section. Pick a chapter or subdeck, study in order or shuffled, or only the cards that mention drugs.
  • Clearly labeled. Imported cards are marked "not reviewed by DrugBug," because they're your own notes.
