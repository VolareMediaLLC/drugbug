Learn your meds by scope.

DrugBug is a medication study app for EMS, nursing, medical school and imaging students, built by a paramedic, FP-C.

Try it free in your browser Coming soon to iPhone

BugDex

All 2,000+ medications in one place, explained in plain language, with drug families compared side by side and illicit and street drug education.

Prehospital

EMT, AEMT, Paramedic and Flight scopes, blood products and hemodynamics.

Nursing

CNA, LVN/LPN and RN, specialty units from ICU and ER to NICU and Flight Nurse.

Medical School

Mechanisms to management, with advanced labs for Step 1, Step 2 CK and Step 3 study.

Imaging

X-ray, CT, MRI, nuclear medicine and IR: contrast agents, reactions, eGFR and radiopharmaceuticals.

Exam prep

NREMT, NCLEX, Step and registry-style practice: single answer, select-all and put-in-priority-order questions, Exam mode, weak-spot drills and a test-day countdown with a daily goal.

My List

Save the drugs you are studying and see, in depth, how they interact with each other.

Compounds and probiotics

The interesting compounds in vegetables, fruits and vitamins, on flip cards with a Surprise me button, plus probiotics.

The Wormhole and the Disease Lab

Go down the wormhole on any drug, or step into the Disease Lab on 250 conditions: where it came from and how it works inside you, from the cell to each organ.

Brain Games and scenarios

Memory match, crossword, scramble and Class Blitz, plus clinical scenarios and Megacode.

3D molecules

See the chemical structure of hundreds of drugs, then tap it to spin a 3D model. Turn the hydrogens on or off and zoom in close.

Grow your bug

Earn sticks for every right answer and build your Terrarium of 90+ bugs, faces and gear.
