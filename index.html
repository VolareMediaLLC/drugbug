Learn your meds by scope.

DrugBug is a medication study app for EMS, nursing, medical school and imaging students, built by a paramedic, FP-C.

Download on the App Store

BugDex

1,350+ medications in one place, explained in plain language, with FDA label sources, drug families side by side, cancer drugs, antivenoms and probiotics.

Prehospital

EMT, AEMT, Paramedic and Flight scopes. Registry-style exam prep with clinical judgment, select-all and put-in-order questions, 52 unfolding calls from scene size-up to handoff, Flight Certification Exam Prep, blood products and hemodynamics.

Nursing

CNA, LVN/LPN and RN, specialty units from ICU and ER to NICU, and licensure-style prep with select-all and ordering questions.

Medical School

Mechanisms to management with Step 1, Step 2 CK and Step 3-style practice, advanced labs, and Body Systems: tap a part of the body to see the drugs that act on it and the drugs that harm it.

Imaging

X-ray, CT, MRI, nuclear medicine and IR: contrast agents, reactions, eGFR and radiopharmaceuticals.

Brain Games

Memory Match, Quick Match, a new med crossword every time, Word Scramble, Class Blitz and Frog Snack.

Grow your bug

Earn sticks for every right answer. Your bug grows through 5 stages, and your Terrarium holds 100+ bugs, faces and gear, including the Giant Hornet and Sky Ace Wasp.
